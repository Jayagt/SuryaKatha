# Surya Katha

**A retelling of Surya Katha as told by Vinay Varanasi.**

Play it here: **https://jayagt.github.io/SuryaKatha/**

Every story in this app, every connection drawn between them, and every śloka
quoted is Vinay Varanasi's — from his lecture on Surya Katha. The telling is his.
This is only a way of walking through it: twelve chapters, each read and then
recalled through a handful of questions.

## What it covers

Twelve chapters, following the lecture's own sequence:

1. Surya in the Scriptures
2. Health from Surya, Wealth from Agni, Knowledge from Īśvara, Liberation from Janārdana
3. Brahmā's Vision of the Light: From Darkness to Sṛṣṭi
4. From Omkāra to Ādityas: Manifestation through Aditi
5. Surya – The Grihasta
6. Surya as Gateway to the Divine
7. Aditya: Space and Time
8. Surya and Yajña: The Divine Transporter
9. Hanuman and Surya – A Leap Towards Light
10. The Power of Usha Kiran – Dawn of Inner Awakening
11. Yājñavalkya and the Grace of Surya – Birth of Two Yajur Vedas
12. Rama and the Aditya Hṛdayam – A Glimpse of Divine Surrender

Still to come: the meaning of the Āditya Hṛdayam.

## Running it

One self-contained file, no build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html`
in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Progress is kept in the browser's own storage, so each reader's place is their
own and nothing is sent anywhere.
